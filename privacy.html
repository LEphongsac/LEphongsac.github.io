Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Personal Video Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data the app accesses

The app uses Google OAuth to get permission to upload videos to YouTube (scope: youtube.upload). It only uses this permission to upload videos and set their details (title, description, tags, privacy).

How data is stored

  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • The app has no server and no database.
  • The app does not collect, sell, or share any personal data.
  • The app does not access anyone else's account or data.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
