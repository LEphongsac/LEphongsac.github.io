Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Personal Video Uploader ("the app", source code: github.com/LEphongsac/toolupyoutube) is a personal command-line tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

Information the app accesses

The app signs in with Google OAuth 2.0 and asks for one permission only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It uses this permission to:

  • upload video files that the user chooses, to the user's own YouTube channel;
  • set the title, description, tags, category and privacy setting of those videos.

The app does not read, collect or analyze any other YouTube or Google account data.

How information is used, stored and shared

  • The OAuth token is stored only on the user's own computer, in a local file (token.json).
  • The app has no server and no database. No data is sent anywhere except directly to Google/YouTube.
  • The app does not sell, share or disclose any information to third parties.
  • The app does not use cookies or any tracking.

Deleting your data

  • Delete the local token.json file to remove all stored login data from your computer immediately.
  • Revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page (https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
  • Videos uploaded with the app can be deleted in YouTube Studio.
  • The app keeps no copy of any data on any server, so there is nothing else to delete. Requests can also be sent to the contact email below and will be handled within 7 days.

Changes to this policy
