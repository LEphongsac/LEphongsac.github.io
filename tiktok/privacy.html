Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

L'Énigme demeure Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

TikTok Platform

The app uses TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API. Use of the app is also subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

The app signs in with TikTok Login Kit and only asks for the scopes needed to publish to the owner's account: user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish. It uses them to:

  • show which TikTok account is signed in (display name and avatar);
  • check the account's allowed privacy options and maximum video length before posting;
  • upload and publish video files that the owner chooses, with their caption, to that account.

The app does not read, collect or analyze any other TikTok data, and does not access other people's profiles, messages, followers or videos.

How information is used, stored and shared

  • The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file.
  • The app has no server and no database. No data is sent anywhere except directly to TikTok.
  • The app does not sell, share or disclose any information to third parties.
  • The app does not use cookies or any tracking.
