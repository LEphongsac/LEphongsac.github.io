L'Énigme demeure Uploader

This is a small personal tool. It publishes videos that I made to my own TikTok account, using the official TikTok Content Posting API.

What it does

  • Signs in to my own TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit.
  • Uploads and publishes my own videos to that account, with their caption and privacy setting.

Who uses it

Only me, the owner of the TikTok account. It is not offered to other users.
