Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Personal Video Uploader for Facebook ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own Facebook Page.

Meta Platform

The app uses the Meta Graph API. Use of the app is also subject to the Meta Terms of Service and the Meta Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

The app signs in with Facebook Login and only asks for the permissions needed to publish to a Page the owner manages, such as pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and pages_manage_posts. It uses them to:

  • find the owner's own Page;
  • publish video files that the owner chooses, with their title and description, to that Page.

The app does not read, collect or analyze any other Facebook data, and does not access other people's profiles, messages or friends.

How information is used, stored and shared

  • The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file.
  • The app has no server and no database. No data is sent anywhere except directly to Meta.
  • The app does not sell, share or disclose any information to third parties.
  • The app does not use cookies or any tracking.
