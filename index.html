Personal Video Uploader

This is a small personal tool. It uploads videos that I made to my own YouTube channel, using the official YouTube Data API.

What it does

  • Uploads my own video files to my own YouTube channel.
  • Sets the title, description, tags and privacy setting of those videos.

Who uses it

Only me, the owner of the channel. It is not offered to other users.
